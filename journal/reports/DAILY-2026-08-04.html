The headline — shorts stopped bleeding on the day the anti-short experiment was watching

Today was the trial's first genuinely short-skewed tape: 6 of 7 signals were shorts, a full inversion of the long-heavy days that built the book so far. And for the first time the short side made money — SHORT 1/3, +₹253, against a cumulative short P&L of -₹2,557 across the whole trial. The single winner, UPL SHORT +₹430 (+1.15%), ran from 09:40 all the way to the 15:10 square-off without ever giving back its trail — the cleanest hold of the week. The one long, LT, was the day's worst trade (-₹137).

That makes today the sharpest test yet of disable_shorts, the experiment that would skip every short. It posted its first losing day: -₹253 (control +₹116 vs treatment -₹137 — i.e. skipping the shorts would have left only the losing long). Cumulative it is still comfortably ahead (+₹1,767 over 4 days), but the "shorts are structurally broken" story now has a real counter-example in the record. Day 4 of 10 — no decision, more evidence.

The one signal that should never have been taken

DIVISLAB SHORT was fired at 09:50 into a stock that had just printed Q1FY27 PAT +65.5% YoY (₹902cr) and was trading +4% on the day. Shorting a fresh, large, positive earnings surprise is exactly the trade skip_contra_catalyst exists to prevent, and it earned +₹89 today by skipping it (cumulative +₹418 over 4 days, day 4 of 10). The loss itself was small (-₹89, -0.27%) — the point is the category, not the size: the breakout logic has no idea a ₹900cr beat just landed, and on a bigger gap that same trade is a multi-₹1,000 loss.

Autopsy: VWAP_REJECTED again — and still no action to take

Two of three losses came back VWAP_REJECTED, the highest-loss-rate cause in the taxonomy (90.4% historical). Two things follow, and neither is a change:

1. The entry gate is inert. The VWAP-alignment gate committed on 07-30 passed all four tracked signals today, and the vwap_alignment experiment logged a ₹0 delta for the third straight day (cumulative ₹0 over 3 days). Every signal was already on the correct side of VWAP at entry; the rejection happened afterwards. A gate that has never once changed an outcome is not the lever.

2. The obvious exit fix is already rejected. "Exit at bar+6 if VWAP was held ≤1/3 of the first six post-entry bars" was simulated against the full 81-week / 1,046-trade backtest on 2026-07-21 and made things worse: portfolio P&L ₹106,331 → ₹89,343 (-16.0%), with the 104 affected trades averaging -₹367 → -₹531. The trailing stop already caps this subset about as well as a fixed-bar exit does, and ~10% of VWAP-rejected trades still recover to a win. Today is consistent with that: all three losses were tiny (-0.13%, -0.27%, -0.31%) because the trail cut them inside 15–125 minutes. The system handled the pattern correctly.

Cumulative autopsy evidence stands at N=39 graded signals, 22 failures, 4 VWAP_REJECTED. Under the cooldown rules this dimension is spent for now — the tally keeps accruing, but nothing here justifies re-opening it.

Verdict

A quietly useful day. Net +₹116 on the tracked book — small, but the first green print since 07-29, and it came from the side of the book everyone had written off. Cumulative v4 trial P&L is -₹1,541 over 54 signals; the long/short split (+₹1,017 long / -₹2,557 short) still says shorts are the problem, but today is the first data point arguing that verdict was reached on too few short-heavy tapes. What is still open, and what today moved, is the short-side question and the contra-catalyst filter. No live fills; Observation Mode continues.
