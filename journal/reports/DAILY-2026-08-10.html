The headline — nothing broke, and that is the finding

The last three postmortems were infrastructure postmortems: a sleep/wake stop on 08-04 and 08-05, a full-session scheduler blackout on 08-07. Today there is nothing to blame. All 40 of 40 scan cycles ran (09:30 → 16:00, logs/scan_all.log), the feed reported LIVE (49 tickers) every cycle, the regime read BULLISH (71% > 20d SMA), and the catalyst scanner covered 6 of 6 tickets before the entry window closed. This is the second clean session in a row and the most complete data capture the trial has had.

The system then took six signals and lost all six, for -₹2,983 net of costs — the worst single day on record, beating 07-30's -₹2,502. Cumulative trial P&L went from -₹3,385 to -₹6,368: 47% of the entire 12-day drawdown was created today. With a clean tape and clean plumbing, the remaining explanation is the strategy itself.

The ORB-width finding has degraded — the "wide is good" half is gone

On 08-06 the width bucket table (n=77) showed ≥1.5% width = 53.1% WR and was called the most predictive dimension found so far. On 08-07 TECHM was the first clean counter-example. Today added three more: PPLPHARMA (2.06%), ABFRL (2.92%) and KAYNES (4.74%) all sat in the favourable bucket and all lost — KAYNES on the widest opening range of the entire trial.

Record is now n=84. The honest restatement: narrow ranges predict failure; wide ranges predict nothing. The wide bucket's win rate barely differs from the middle bucket's and its P&L is worse, because the losses in it are large — a wide opening range means a wide trailing stop, so when a wide breakout fails it costs multiples of what a narrow failure costs (KAYNES -2.07%, ABFRL -2.90%). Any future width proposal must be tested jointly with a condition that caps that downside; width alone is not a filter.

vwap_alignment is structurally incapable of producing evidence

Seven recorded days, 32 graded signals, zero skips, delta identically ₹0 every single day. That is not a null result — it is a broken experiment, and today's data shows exactly why.

The VWAP-alignment rule was already committed to the live scanner (commit e2bac74). scan_all.py assigns a misaligned breakout the status ENTRY-VWAP-BLOCK and routes it to watch-only, so it never becomes an ENTRY. The experiment engine (scripts/exp_vwap_alignment.py) reads the VWAP- flag off ENTRY/ENTRY-LATE rows only. The rule it is testing has already removed its own test population. Control and treatment are the same set by construction, and running it to day 10 will produce ₹0 with certainty.

Today also shows what the entry-alignment gate does not catch. All six entries passed it — none carried a VWAP- flag at entry. Yet the autopsy graded INDHOTEL as VWAP_REJECTED: it held the correct side of VWAP for only 17% of the trade (vwap_hold 0.17), a cause with a 90.4% prior loss rate. Alignment at the entry bar and alignment through the trade are different variables, and only the first one is gated.

Recommendation (propose-only, no action taken): close vwap_alignment as already-committed — the same disposition orb_entry_confirmation_bar received — rather than letting it run out its remaining days to a guaranteed zero. If the VWAP idea is worth retesting, it should be re-registered against VWAP hold (exit when price loses VWAP for N consecutive bars), which is an unmeasured dimension, not a re-run of a rule already in force. Registry status is unchanged pending human approval.

Verdict

The trial has now had two consecutive clean sessions, and both were losses — today decisively so. With the infrastructure excuses gone, the evidence points one way: the v4 ruleset takes too many signals, and the ones it should not take are where the money goes. The short book is -₹3,671 on 6/17 for the whole trial; disable_shorts would have returned +₹2,881 of the -₹6,368 drawdown, and it is two days from a decision. skip_contra_catalyst caught the day's second-worst trade.

Nothing is committed on the strength of one bad day — the protocol's minimum-N exists precisely for days like this. But the direction of both running experiments, the collapse of the width edge, and the fact that the VWAP experiment can never report, together say the next real decision point is 2 sessions away and it will be about trading less, not about filtering better.
