The headline — longs went 0/4 into a market that drifted up

What makes it a real signal rather than a bad tape: the market was mildly positive. ⚠

All scanners stopped at 11:00 IST — 4h15m of the session never scanned

scan_all.log and orb_scan.log both end at the 11:00 cycle; the Kotak feed ran to 12:14 and then stopped too. Nothing crashed — the logs end mid-session with a clean summary block, which points at the host sleeping or Task Scheduler dropping the jobs rather than a code fault. 25 scan cycles (11:10 → 15:10) were lost, along with any signal that fired in the 11:00–11:30 ORB entry window.

Recovered. A new replay tool, scripts/backfill_missed_cycles.py, re-runs the live check_orb() against the 5-min history cache with the data sliced to what was knowable at each cycle timestamp — a faithful reconstruction, not a hindsight backtest. It restored 49 timeline checkpoints and found one missed recommendation, HDFCLIFE LONG @ 11:20. Validation against the last surviving live cycle (11:00) reproduced the same tickers, directions and statuses; only the price differs (₹174.81 vs ₹174.97 on GAIL) because the replay uses the completed 5-min bar where the live scanner saw a partial one. Every replayed row is stamped "backfilled": true.

✓

RESOLVED: yesterday's HDFCBANK gap was never a logging bug — recommendations.jsonl has two producers

The 08-04 journal left this open as "_log_signal_timeline stopped emitting after ~09:50". That diagnosis was wrong. scan_all.log ran to 12:20 that day and logged LT, UPL, DIVISLAB continuously — the writer was healthy.

The real cause is architectural: two independent scanners write recommendations, only one writes the timeline. scan_all.py (150 tickers, 10-min cadence) journals proposals and feeds signal_timeline.jsonl. scripts/task_orb_propose.py via scan_orb_live (Nifty-50 only, 15-min cadence, stricter vol+VWAP+RSI gates) journals proposals and never touches the timeline at all. HDFCBANK SHORT @10:00, BAJAJ-AUTO @11:15 and ADANIPORTS @11:30 were all produced by the second scanner — scan_all never emitted an ORB row for HDFCBANK in any cycle, so there was nothing to log. Any signal unique to the ORB-propose task is structurally invisible to the price tracker and silently dropped from tracked P&L.

Today is clean on this axis by luck: orb_scan posted Entries: 0 in all seven of its cycles, so all five of today's signals came from scan_all.

DIAGNOSED — the fix (have the ORB-propose task emit timeline rows, or make the tracker fall back to a bar replay for timeline-less recommendations) is a code change, not a strategy change. Proposed, not applied.
