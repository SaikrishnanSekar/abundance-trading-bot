The "shorts are structurally broken" thesis is taking sustained fire.

APLAPOLLO — the carve-out that let a blow-off through

APLAPOLLO fired at RSI 81 on 7.8x volume with the narrowest opening range of the day (0.38%) — the textbook profile of an exhausted chase, not a breakout. It was trail-stopped five minutes later for -₹176.

The rsi_overbought_entry experiment did not skip it, and that is by design, not a bug: the rule is catalyst-aware — skip a long at RSI ≥ 80 only if the catalyst is not positive. APLAPOLLO's catalyst was positive (Q1 PAT +10.9%, stock already +6.6% on the day), so the carve-out exempted it and the experiment logged a ₹0 delta.

That carve-out just cost ₹176 of measurable evidence. The stock being up 6.6% already is precisely why RSI was 81 — the "supporting catalyst" and the "overbought" condition are the same fact counted twice. Worth noting for the day-10 review, now one session away.

Second-order note: the two replay engines disagree on this trade. journal.orb_autopsy reports "signal never triggered in bars" and does not grade it, while the price tracker fills it and books -₹176. Same disagreement as LICI on 08-05. The tracker's number is the one in the day's P&L.

The finding of the trial: opening-range width predicts the outcome, and v4 deleted that gate

Today's four signals ranked perfectly by opening-range width — the two widest won and held to the 15:10 square-off, the two narrowest were trail-stopped inside 20 minutes: AUROPHARMA 2.34% → +₹120, BIOCON 1.74% → +₹313, TVSMOTOR 0.68% → -₹274, APLAPOLLO 0.38% → -₹176. That prompted a sweep of every signal with a logged width across the whole record (n=77), and the relationship is monotonic:

Collapsed at the 1.00% line: width ≥ 1.00% → n=50, 52.0% WR, +₹2,433. Width < 1.00% → n=27, 22.2% WR, -₹1,384. The narrow-range signals are not merely worse — they are the entire drawdown, and the wide ones are quietly profitable underneath them.

ORB v4 removed the width gate on 2026-07-19 ("only degenerate ranges skipped", <0.10%). v3 had required ≥1.5%. On this record that removal looks like the single largest self-inflicted wound of the trial. Note the gate does not have to come back at v3's 1.5% — the data says the damage is concentrated below 1.0%, and the 1.00–1.50% band is the best bucket of all, which a 1.5% gate would have thrown away.

Two supporting cuts from the same sweep: (1) more volume is worse, not better — 2–3x is the strongest bucket (n=40, +₹2,729) while ≥5x blow-off entries are negative (n=9, -₹550); APLAPOLLO's 7.8x was a warning, not a confirmation. (2) The trail stop is doing all the work — signals held to the 15:10 square-off run 71.8% WR / +₹8,407, while trail-stopped signals run 10.5% WR / -₹7,358. Once the trail fires, recovery essentially does not happen.

Status: not a proposal yet. n=77 clears the 20-trade structural minimum, but the width dimension must go through scripts/proposal-check.sh for cooldown (v4's own width decision was 2026-07-19) and then run as a registered controlled experiment before any TRADING-STRATEGY.md commit. Recorded here as evidence, not as a change.
