## What WORKED

- **LODHA — real earnings catalyst + volume + held VWAP.** The only recommendation
  with a fresh positive company-specific catalyst (Q1 profit +103%). Broke out,
  held above VWAP the entire session, and *accelerated into the close* (+1.3% in the
  final hour). This is the template: hard catalyst → sustained trend.
- **CHOLAFIN — genuine Q1 + sector momentum, best relative volume.** Reclaimed VWAP
  after an early dip on the batch's strongest follow-through volume. Faded late on
  profit-taking near its all-time high but stayed green.

## What did NOT work (root cause traced per name)

- **IT cluster (HCLTECH RSI 89, TCS 86, OFSS 79) — borrowed sector momentum, no own
  catalyst.** All three gapped on the Nifty-IT rally (+9% off the Jul 1 low, driven by
  TCS's Q1 beat), entered badly overbought, and had **no company-specific news of their
  own**. Volume collapsed on the bars right after the breakout (0.02–0.19× the breakout
  bar) and all three drifted to/below VWAP. Momentum was already spent.
- **SAIL (RSI 72) — hard NEGATIVE catalyst.** Reported a weak Q1 (profit & revenue down
  sequentially). Gapped up on metal-sector momentum at the open; the ORB fired long into
  a weak print, lost VWAP immediately, and stayed red open-to-close. An ORB long placed
  directly against fresh bad fundamentals.
- **ABFRL (RSI 81) — no catalyst + overbought.** Ran on a restructuring story with
  earnings not due until Jul 31. Entered overbought with nothing fresh to sustain it;
  volume evaporated and it made new session lows.

## The single traceable pattern

Every move maps cleanly onto **catalyst quality × VWAP hold**:
- **Winners:** fresh positive company-specific catalyst AND price held above VWAP → LODHA, CHOLAFIN.
- **Losers:** no own catalyst (or a *negative* one) AND price lost VWAP → TCS, OFSS, SAIL, ABFRL; HCLTECH flat.
- **RSI ≥ 80 at entry** was present on 3 of the 4 clean failures (ABFRL 81, HCLTECH 89, TCS 86)
  — a useful *risk flag*, but **confounded**: CHOLAFIN (85) was a winner and LODHA (72) the
  best trade. Catalyst is the dominant variable; RSI-overbought is a secondary proxy the
  scanner can compute cheaply, unlike catalyst.
