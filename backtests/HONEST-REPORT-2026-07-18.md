## Plain-language conclusion (as required by the spec)

The achievable probability that a current-logic recommendation returns 3–4%
within a week is **roughly 1 in 4 — not near-certainty, and no better than
chance**. Trading the MA-abundance swing signals live for this goal would lose
money after costs. No selection logic in this repo currently produces a
positive-expectancy 3–4%-in-5-days profile on real data. Any claim otherwise
would be an overstated-confidence failure (P0 per the build spec).

What the evidence DOES support:
- **ORB intraday sleeve** remains the only real-data positive strategy
  (94 trades, 56 days: 63.8% WR, avg R 1.31, +₹6,167 on ₹20k ≈ ~2.7%/week on
  cash — backtest, zero live trades logged yet).
- **The path to a better swing hit rate is now mechanical**: the trade journal
  records every recommendation with its exact signal values; the feedback loop
  will surface any signal whose with/without hit-rate difference survives a
  significance test (Gate 2) AND out-of-sample confirmation (Gate 3), at N≥30
  (Gate 1), for human approval (Gate 4), with rollback monitoring (Gate 5).
